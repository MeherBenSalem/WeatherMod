# Changelog

Release notes for each version are in [patchnotes/](patchnotes).

## 1.0.0

First release, for Minecraft 1.20.1 (Fabric and Forge) and, new, for Minecraft 1.21.1, 26.2 and 26.3 (Fabric and
NeoForge). On 1.21.1 the water, plant sway and seasonal colours are not drawn on terrain rendered by Sodium. On 26.2
and 26.3 the shader-based visuals (see-through water, 3D clouds, sky and fog colours, plant sway, the atmosphere
effects, raindrops on the view, glowing footprints and tumbling dropped items) are not available; weather, seasons,
footprints, sound and gameplay are the same. See [patchnotes/1.0.0.md](patchnotes/1.0.0.md).
