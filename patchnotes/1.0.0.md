# Living World 1.0.0

The first release.

## Supported versions

- **Minecraft 1.20.1** on Fabric and Forge.
- **Minecraft 1.21.1** on Fabric and NeoForge (new).
- **Minecraft 26.2** on Fabric and NeoForge (new).
- **Minecraft 26.3** on Fabric and NeoForge (new).

Fabric needs Fabric API; Mod Menu is optional. Forge and NeoForge need nothing else.

## What it does

One shared simulation of the air around you (temperature, humidity, wind, rain, ground wetness, shelter) that every
effect reads from: weather that builds and clears, four seasons, wind-driven rain and snow, footprints that the
weather wears away, frost at the edge of your view in the cold, camera sway in strong wind, a synthesised soundscape,
and opt-in server rules for real winter snow and seasonal crop growth. Press F9 or run `/lw config` for the settings.

## What differs between versions

- **1.20.1** has everything, including the Sodium (Fabric) and Embeddium (Forge) support for the water, plant sway
  and seasonal colours.
- **1.21.1** has everything on the vanilla renderer. With Sodium installed, the water shading, plant sway and
  seasonal leaf colours are missing from the terrain Sodium draws.
- **26.2 and 26.3** use the game's new renderer, which has no place for the mod's own shaders. Not drawn there:
  the see-through water (reflections, caustics, surf and water colour), the 3D clouds and their shadows, the sky and
  fog colours, swaying plants and seasonal leaf colours, the atmosphere effects (heat haze, mist, wet ground and
  puddles, light shafts, colour grading, bloom, film look, vignette, adjusting to brightness), raindrops on the
  view, the warm and cold screen tint, glowing footprints at night (prints are still drawn), and tumbling dropped
  items. The sky only flashes when you can see the lightning bolt. The weather, seasons, wind, rain, snow,
  particles, footprints, frost, sound, camera effects, commands and seasonal rules work as everywhere else. The
  settings for the missing effects are hidden on these versions.
